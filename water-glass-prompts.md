# Prompty do Gemini — Szklanka wody ucieka od ściany

## 0% — EMPTY GLASS
A comic style illustration. A completely empty glass of water stands against a brick wall. The glass has a sad face expression. Everything is monochrome/grey. One tiny crack in the wall behind it. The atmosphere is depressing, like a prisoner in a cell. Text at bottom: "0% — ..." No bright colors, only greys and browns. Minimalist style.

## 10% — ONE DROP
The same glass of water now has one tiny sip of water at the bottom. The glass has taken one small step away from the wall. It looks back at the wall with a slightly hopeful expression. Still mostly grey but a tiny hint of blue in the water drop. Text: "10% — maybe?" Minimalist comic style.

## 30% — 1/3 FULL
The glass is filled to about 30% with clear water. It has walked halfway across the room toward an open door. The glass looks determined, not sad anymore. Some color is returning — the water is blue, the glass has some shine. The wall behind it has more cracks. Text: "30% — time to go." Minimalist comic style.

## 50% — HALF FULL
The glass is half full of water. It stands at the open door, one metaphorical foot out. The glass looks back over its shoulder at the cracked wall with a confident smirk. Colors are fully vibrant now — blue water, golden light coming through the door. Text: "50% — halfway there." Minimalist comic style.

## 70% — MORE THAN HALF
The glass is 70% full. It has one foot outside the door, only half its body still inside. The glass is smiling now. Behind it, the wall is crumbling. Bright warm light comes from outside. Text: "70% — almost free." Minimalist comic style.

## 90% — ALMOST FULL
The glass is 90% full. Only the back edge of the glass is still visible — most of it is already outside the frame. The glass looks happy and free. The wall in the background is falling apart, bricks floating. Text: "90% — so close." Minimalist comic style.

## 100% — COMPLETELY FULL
The glass is completely full to the brim with water. It has turned around completely, facing away from the viewer, walking toward a bright horizon. One hand in the air giving a middle finger back at the crumbled wall. Speech bubble: "Fuck it!" The wall is completely destroyed in the background. Golden sunlight floods the scene. Text: "100% — free at last." Minimalist comic style.
